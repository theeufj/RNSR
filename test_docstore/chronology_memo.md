## Key Issues and Notes

### Liability
- **Strong case.** Police report confirms Thornton ran red light. Thornton admitted to being distracted by his phone (noted in police officer's observations, though not a formal admission). SCATS camera footage obtained under GIPA request — clearly shows Thornton entering on red.
- Witness Marcus Wong provides corroborating account.
- No contributory negligence issues identified.

### Quantum
- **Past economic loss:** Straightforward to prove with payslips and employer records. GreenField has been cooperative.
- **Future economic loss:** Client's employer has flagged that full-time return is required by 30 June 2025. If client cannot return full-time, may need to find alternative employment at reduced capacity. Consider vocational assessment.
- **General damages:** 15% WPI places claim in the upper bracket for District Court. Consider comparable decisions:
  - *Roberts v NRMA* [2023] NSWDC 412 — similar injuries, $165,000 general damages
  - *Patel v Allianz* [2024] NSWDC 89 — femur fracture + PTSD, $190,000 general damages
- **Future medical:** Nail removal surgery is certain. Shoulder surgery is contingent — may need to discount.

### Risks
1. Thornton may deny phone use — police noted "distracted by phone" in observations but did not issue a mobile phone infringement. May need to subpoena phone records.
2. Shoulder injury: NRMA may argue pre-existing degenerative change. MRI shows no pre-existing degeneration, but may need to address in supplementary report.
3. PTSD: NRMA may seek their own psychiatric assessment. Dr. Park's diagnosis appears solid — she notes specific DSM-5 criteria met.
