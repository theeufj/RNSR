- Invoices rendered monthly

## 5. Conditional Costs Agreement

This matter is conducted on a **partial no-win, no-fee basis**:
- If the claim is unsuccessful, professional fees are waived
- Disbursements remain payable by the Client regardless of outcome
- If successful, the Firm is entitled to charge an uplift of **25%** on professional fees
- The maximum uplift permitted under the Legal Profession Uniform Law is 25%

## 6. Client's Right to Negotiate

The Client has the right to:
- Negotiate the costs disclosed in this agreement
- Seek independent legal advice about the costs
- Request an itemised bill
- Apply to have costs assessed by a costs assessor

## 7. Cooling-Off Period

The Client has **5 business days** from the date of signing this agreement to withdraw without penalty.
