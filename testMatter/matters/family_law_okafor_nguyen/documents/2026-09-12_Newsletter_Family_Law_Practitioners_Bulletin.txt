ANNUAL DINNER
Our annual dinner will be held at the Regent Ballroom on Friday 23 October 2026. Tickets are $165 for members and $195 for guests. The guest speaker is the Hon. Margaret Linthwaite AO, who will speak about thirty years in the family courts. Dress is cocktail attire. Please book by 9 October.

CPD SEMINAR
On Wednesday 21 October from 5:30pm, Dr Alan Brookes will present a two-point CPD seminar on valuing small businesses and family trusts in property settlements. Attendance is free for members. Register at the Bulletin website.

PRACTICE TIP
Remember that a financial statement must be updated if there is a material change before the hearing. A short supplementary affidavit is usually enough.
