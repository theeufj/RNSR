INNER WEST COMMUNITY NEWSLETTER - October 2025 (fictional)
Issue 41. Published by the Marrickville and Dulwich Hill Neighbourhood Association.

WHAT'S ON THIS MONTH
Saturday 4 October: Spring Street Market on Illawarra Road, 8 am to 2 pm. Over 60 stalls, including fresh produce, vintage clothing and food trucks. Live music from the Dulwich Hill Brass Collective from 11 am.

Wednesday 8 October: Library talk, "Growing herbs on a balcony", Marrickville Library, 6:30 pm. Free entry; bookings recommended on 02 5550 0311.

Saturday 18 October: Community clean-up day at the Cooks River foreshore. Meet at the picnic area near the footbridge at 9 am. Gloves and bags supplied.

COUNCIL NOTICES
Bin collection days for the 2203 postcode will not change over the long weekend. Residents are reminded that bulky waste pick-ups must be booked at least 7 days ahead. The council has also resurfaced Wattle Grove Road between Hill Street and the railway line; expect minor delays until 14 October.

LOCAL BUSINESS SPOTLIGHT
Corner Cafe on Marrickville Road celebrates its tenth anniversary with a free pastry for the first 50 customers each Friday in October. The owners thank customers for their loyal custom over the decade.

VOLUNTEERS WANTED
The neighbourhood association needs volunteers for the December carols evening. Contact the committee secretary on 0400 000 905 or at newsletter@example.com.au.
