I, **Sarah Mitchell**, of 42 Wattle Avenue, Homebush NSW 2140, Accounts Manager, affirm:

### Background

1. I am the Plaintiff in these proceedings. I make this affidavit in support of my claim for damages arising from a motor vehicle accident on **22 January 2025**.

2. I am employed by **GreenField Logistics Pty Ltd** as an Accounts Manager. I have held this position since March 2021. My annual salary at the time of the accident was **$92,000** plus superannuation.

### The Accident

3. On 22 January 2025, at approximately **8:15 AM**, I was driving my vehicle (a 2022 Toyota Corolla, registration NSW plate DCL-78P) southbound on George Street, Sydney.

4. I was travelling at approximately 40 km/h, which was the posted speed limit. Traffic was moderate. The weather was clear and the road surface was dry.

5. As I approached the intersection with Park Road, the traffic light was green in my direction. I proceeded through the intersection.

6. The First Defendant, **James Thornton**, was driving a white 2019 Ford Ranger (registration NSW plate BXK-42F) westbound on Park Road. He entered the intersection against a red traffic light and collided with the driver's side of my vehicle.

7. The impact was severe. My vehicle was pushed approximately 4 metres sideways. The driver's side door was crushed inward. Airbags deployed.

8. I was trapped in the vehicle for approximately **25 minutes** until NSW Fire and Rescue extracted me using hydraulic cutting equipment. I was conscious throughout but in significant pain.

### Injuries

9. I was transported by ambulance to Royal Prince Alfred Hospital, where I was admitted to the Emergency Department at approximately 9:05 AM.

10. I sustained the following injuries:
    - **Fractured left femur** (mid-shaft, requiring surgical fixation with intramedullary nail on 23 January 2025, performed by Dr. Amanda Chen)
    - **Three fractured ribs** (left side, ribs 5-7)
    - **Left shoulder rotator cuff tear** (partial thickness)
    - **Cervical spine soft tissue injury** (whiplash)
    - **Multiple lacerations** to the left arm and face from shattered glass
    - **Psychological injury** — diagnosed with Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) by Dr. Helen Park, psychiatrist, on 15 March 2025

11. I was hospitalised for **12 days** (22 January to 3 February 2025).

### Treatment and Recovery

12. Since discharge, I have attended:
    - **Dr. Amanda Chen** (orthopaedic surgeon) — 8 follow-up appointments
    - **PhysioWorks Homebush** — 24 physiotherapy sessions (ongoing, twice weekly)
    - **Dr. Helen Park** (psychiatrist) — 10 sessions (ongoing, fortnightly)
    - **Dr. James Wu** (GP) — monthly reviews for pain management

13. I used crutches for **10 weeks** following surgery. I now walk with a noticeable limp and experience daily pain in my left leg, rated 4-6/10 on most days.

14. Dr. Chen has advised that I will likely require a **further surgery** (removal of the intramedullary nail) in approximately 12 months. She has also indicated I may develop early-onset osteoarthritis in the left knee due to the altered gait.

### Impact on Employment

15. I was unable to work from 22 January 2025 until **14 April 2025** (approximately 12 weeks). I returned to work on a graduated return-to-work plan: 3 days per week for the first 4 weeks, then 4 days per week.

16. As at the date of this affidavit, I am working 4 days per week. My employer has been accommodating but has indicated that my position requires full-time attendance, and they cannot guarantee the current arrangement beyond 30 June 2025.

17. My income loss to date is approximately **$23,000** (gross). I continue to suffer ongoing income reduction of approximately **$460 per week** (gross) due to reduced hours.

### Impact on Daily Life

18. Before the accident, I was an active person. I played social netball (Saturday mornings), regularly ran 5km three times per week, and enjoyed bushwalking with my partner.

19. I am currently unable to participate in any of these activities. Dr. Chen has advised that I should not expect to return to running. I may be able to resume light walking in 6-12 months.

20. I require assistance with household tasks including vacuuming, mopping, gardening, and carrying groceries. My partner, Thomas Garcia, provides approximately **10 hours per week** of domestic assistance that I previously performed myself.

21. I experience significant anxiety when driving or travelling as a passenger. I have not driven since the accident. I rely on public transport and ride-share services to commute to work. Dr. Park has linked this driving anxiety to my PTSD diagnosis.

### Expenses Incurred

22. I have incurred the following out-of-pocket expenses to date:
    - Medical and surgical: $8,450
    - Physiotherapy: $4,320
    - Psychiatry: $3,200
    - Medications: $890
    - Transport (Uber to appointments): $1,240
    - Domestic assistance (commercial rate equivalent): $5,200
    - Vehicle towing and storage: $650
    - **Total: $23,950**

23. My vehicle was assessed as a total loss. The insured value was $28,000. I received $26,500 from my insurer after the excess. The replacement vehicle I purchased cost $31,000, resulting in a net loss of **$4,500**.
