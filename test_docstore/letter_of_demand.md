Dear Sir/Madam,

We act on behalf of **Sarah Mitchell** in relation to her claim for damages arising from a motor vehicle accident on 22 January 2025.

### Liability

Our client was driving southbound on George Street, Sydney, when your insured, **James Thornton** (vehicle registration BXK-42F), ran a red traffic light at the intersection of George Street and Park Road and collided with our client's vehicle.

We enclose:
1. NSW Police Accident Report (Event No. E89012345) confirming the First Defendant ran the red light
2. Traffic camera footage from Sydney Coordinated Adaptive Traffic System (SCATS) camera GS-PR-004
3. Witness statement of Marcus Wong (pedestrian at the intersection)

We submit that **liability rests entirely with your insured** and there is no contributory negligence on the part of our client.

### Injuries and Treatment

Our client sustained serious injuries as set out in the enclosed medical evidence:
- Fractured left femur (surgically fixated, Dr. Amanda Chen report enclosed)
- Three fractured ribs (left side)
- Partial rotator cuff tear (left shoulder)
- Cervical spine soft tissue injury
- Multiple lacerations
- Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (Dr. Helen Park report enclosed)

Treatment is ongoing. A further surgery (nail removal) is anticipated within 12 months. Dr. Chen's prognosis indicates a **15% whole person impairment (WPI)** for the left leg and a likely permanent limp.
