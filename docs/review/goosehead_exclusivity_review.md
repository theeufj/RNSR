# Independent verification: Goosehead exclusivity question

**Contract:** GOOSEHEADINSURANCE,INC_04_02_2018-EX-10.6-Franchise Agreement (Franchise Agreement, SEC exhibit; 300,768 chars). Full text: `goosehead_contract.txt` in this folder.

**Question asked:** Highlight the parts (if any) of this contract related to "Exclusivity" that should be reviewed by a lawyer. Details: Is there an exclusive dealing  commitment with the counterparty? This includes a commitment to procure all “requirements” from one party of certain technology, goods, or services or a prohibition on licensing or selling technology, goods or services to third parties, or a prohibition on  collaborating or working with other parties), whether during the contract or  after the contract ends (or both). If the contract contains no such clause, answer exactly: No such clause.

**CUAD gold:** No such clause  (i.e. the annotators marked NO exclusivity clause)

## docdb answered (WRONG per gold)

This contract contains multiple exclusivity provisions that should be reviewed by a lawyer:

1. **Exclusive Activity Restriction (Section 8.10)**: If the franchisee is an entity (corporation, LLC, or partnership), it must "confine [its] activities...exclusively to operating the Franchised Business." This is an exclusive dealing commitment requiring the entity to engage only in the franchised business and no other business activities.

2. **Covenant Not to Compete (Section 19.3)**: The franchisee covenants not to compete both during the term of the agreement AND for two (2) years after expiration or termination. This covenant prohibits the franchisee from directly or indirectly, for themselves or through any other party, engaging in certain prohibited activities including: (a) diverting or attempting to divert any business or customer to any competitor, and (b) employing or seeking to employ any person employed by the franchisor or other franchisees.

These provisions constitute exclusive dealing commitments and prohibitions on competing or collaborating with third parties both during and after the contract term.

## rlm-classic answered (WRONG per gold)

The following parts of this contract related to "Exclusivity" should be reviewed by a lawyer:

**Section 7.1 - Required and Approved Products and Services**
This section requires the franchisee to purchase or lease certain products and services only from the franchisor, its affiliates, or approved suppliers. The franchisor has the right to be the exclusive approved supplier of certain products and services, and can revoke approval of any supplier at any time.

**Section 8.4 - No Competing Business (During Term)**
During the term of the Agreement, the franchisee and its principals are prohibited from directly or indirectly owning, operating, engaging in, being employed by, providing services to, or having any financial interest in any Competing Business. "Competing Business" is defined as: (a) any business that offers or provides insurance products or services; or (b) any business that operates under a franchise or license agreement with any person or entity other than the franchisor. This restriction applies worldwide.

**Section 8.5 - Exclusive Dealing**
The franchisee must offer and sell ONLY those insurance products and services that the franchisor has approved in writing. The franchisee must use ONLY those insurance carriers and managing general agents that the franchisor has approved in writing. The franchisor may revoke approval at any time and for any reason, requiring immediate cessation of use.

**Section 19.2 - Covenant Not to Compete During the Term**
Reiterates the prohibition during the term against the franchisee and its principals participating in any Competing Business in any capacity, anywhere in the world.

**Section 19.3 - Covenant Not to Compete After Termination or Expiration**
For TWO (2) YEARS after termination or expiration, the franchisee and its principals are prohibited from participating in any Competing Business: (a) at the Approved Location; (b) within a 10-mile radius of the Approved Location; and (c) within a 10-mile radius of any other Goosehead Insurance Agency business operating or under development at the time of termination.

These provisions establish comprehensive exclusive dealing commitments including: (1) a requirement to procure all insurance products/services and use only approved carriers/agents; (2) prohibition on engaging in competing insurance businesses during the contract term (worldwide); and (3) prohibition on competing for 2 years after contract termination (within specified geographic areas).
